# Chorus
> A workspace where multiple LLMs speak at once — you synthesize the rest.

## What is Chorus?
A multi-LLM workspace. Send one prompt to multiple models, 
compare answers side by side, and build something better 
than any single model could give you.

## Why Chorus?
Most people who use AI seriously don't use just one model.
But there's no good way to use them together — until now.
